One outlet or transfer talk not yet backed by a second major outlet. Treat as unconfirmed.

    How this works

    Twice a day we read the United feeds of BBC Sport, Sky Sports, The Athletic, ESPN, the Manchester Evening News, the Evening Standard, Football365, 90min, the Mail, the Mirror and three fan sites, keep the last 36 hours and group articles about the same story, so each story appears once with every outlet that covered it. Confirmed: two or more major outlets. Reported: at least one. Rumour: transfer talk or a single tabloid or fan-site claim. Headlines and short standfirsts belong to the outlets; follow the links to read the full articles.
