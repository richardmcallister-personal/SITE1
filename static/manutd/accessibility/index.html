Accessibility statement

manutd.wiki aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, on phones and computers, in light and dark mode and in every language the site offers.

What the site does

  • The site is built to work with a keyboard: focus is shown with a clear outline, and every page starts with a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Buttons and links on phones have tap areas of about 44 × 44 pixels where practical.
  • Text and controls are checked for colour contrast in light and dark mode; you can pick either in the footer, or follow your device.
  • Pages reflow from wide screens down to phone width without sideways scrolling, and keep their content in a sensible reading order.
  • Animations are switched off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Ask can be used by voice and can read its answers aloud.

How it was checked

On 7 November 2026, 20 key pages were scanned with an automated WCAG 2.2 A and AA checker (axe-core 4.10) in light and dark mode at 1280 and 390 pixels wide, and the issues it found were fixed. Keyboard use of the header, match actions and navigation was checked by hand, and the layout was reviewed at 375, 390 and 430 pixels wide. The site has not had a full manual audit or testing with screen-reader users, so it does not claim full conformance.

Known issues

  • On Trips, a few away fixtures shown in a club’s own colours fall just short of the contrast target (4.47:1 against 4.5:1).
  • On Who wore this number?, the band of holders puts links inside a scrolling control, and the small dots for each holder are under 24 pixels on phones.
  • The appearance switch in the footer has small buttons.
  • Content from other services, such as embedded YouTube videos, the stadium seat map and linked websites, is outside the site’s control.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site is hard to use, email us with the page and what happened.
