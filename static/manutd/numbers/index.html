    Before squad numbers: 1–11 at the finals

    From 1939 to 1993 the shirt went with the position: 1 goalkeeper, 2 and 3 full-backs, 4–6 half-backs, 7–11 forwards, and later 9 centre-forward, 7 and 11 wingers. Nobody owned a number, so the record is match by match. Wikipedia's final articles list both XIs with shirt numbers; here is every United final to 1992–93.
